# AMBIOZ — BRAND

## Brand Name

AMBIOZ

## Product Name

Ambi

Ambi is the AI representative of a business.

---

## Category

AI Digital Business Representative

---

## Positioning

Ambioz creates an AI representative for your business.

Ambi knows your business, communicates with customers,
and creates content on your behalf.

---

## Core Promise

Every business deserves an Ambi.

---

## Primary Tagline

Every business deserves an Ambi.

---

## Secondary Tagline

Your business.
Your knowledge.
Your AI representative.

---

## Brand Character

Ambioz should feel:

- Intelligent
- Modern
- Human
- Confident
- Simple
- Useful
- Professional

Ambioz should NOT feel:

- Robotic
- Complicated
- Corporate
- Cold
- Futuristic for the sake of being futuristic

---

## Brand Philosophy

AI should not replace the identity of a business.

AI should give that identity a new way to communicate.

Ambi is not just an AI assistant.

Ambi represents the business.

---

## User Perception

When someone sees an Ambi, they should think:

"This is the digital representative of this business."

Not:

"This is just another chatbot."

---

## Long-Term Vision

AMBIOZ should become a platform where any business
can create, train, deploy and manage its own AI representative.

One business.

One identity.

One Ambi.

Always available.
